# Code of conduct

This library exists so that people from rival tendencies can work on the same texts and argue in the open. That only works with a few firm rules.

## Argue with positions, not people

Disagree as hard as the argument deserves. Do not attack, mock or threaten the person making it. Sectarian baiting ("revisionist", "lib", "tankie", "anarkiddie" used as insults rather than as arguments) is not argument.

## Represent others fairly

Before criticising another tradition, describe its position the way its own adherents would recognise. Name traditions as their adherents name themselves.

## Source your claims

Claims about what a text says cite verses. Claims about history cite sources. That includes claims about repression, famine, war and mass violence under any government or movement; dismissing documented victims is not commentary.

## Respect each tradition's space

Each tradition's files in `commentary/` and `renderings/` are maintained by that tradition. Answer with your own entry; do not edit theirs. Core maintainers check format, sources and conduct, not doctrine.

## Keep the original honest

The shared modern draft in `works/` translates; it does not take sides. Pushing a tendency's reading into the shared draft, rather than into commentary or a rendering, will be reverted.

## Not allowed

Harassment, hate speech, doxxing, calls for violence against people, and spam. These get one warning and then a ban, or an immediate ban if severe.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a private message to a maintainer or by emailing the address on the repository owner's profile. Maintainers will act in proportion and say what they did.
