# Sources: Value, Price and Profit (VPP)

## Original column (English)

Marx wrote this paper in English and read it to the General Council of the International Working Men's Association on 20 and 27 June 1865, answering John Weston, who had argued that wage rises are useless because prices simply follow. The manuscript was published only in 1898, edited by Eleanor Marx Aveling with a preface by Edward Aveling. Section titles are Eleanor Marx's. The 1898 text is in the public domain.

**Text used:** the Marxists Internet Archive transcription (HTML mark-up by Mike Ballard 1995, proofed and corrected by Brandon Poole 2009; MIA transcriptions are CC BY-SA), files `preface.html`, `ch01.html`, `ch02.html`, `ch03.html` from the mirror at `github.com/MARX-EBOOKS/marx-ebooks.github.io`, path `en/archive/marx/works/1865/value-price-profit/`.

**Left out:** MIA's introduction (taken from a 1969 International Publishers edition, possibly protected), MIA's endnotes, and one MIA square-bracket gloss in section I ("[The aristocracy was the upper class of Great Britain, while the capitalists composed what was known to Marx as the middle class]"). The gloss is a good starting point for a footnote in `commentary/general/`.

**No translation column.** The original is English. MEW 16, pp. 101-152, prints a German translation by the MEW editors; it is not an original and is not included.

## Sections

`P1898` is Aveling's preface. `0` is Marx's untitled preliminary remarks. `I` to `XIV` are the numbered sections. Each section's `0` paragraph is its title.
