# Sources: The German Ideology (GI)

## German column

**Text.** Marx and Engels, *Die deutsche Ideologie. Kritik der neuesten deutschen Philosophie in ihren Repräsentanten Feuerbach, B. Bauer und Stirner, und des deutschen Sozialismus in seinen verschiedenen Propheten*, written in Brussels between autumn 1845 and summer 1846. It found no publisher. Parts appeared in their lifetime (chapter IV of volume II in 1847); the whole was first printed in 1932 by the Marx-Engels Institute in Moscow. The base text is MEW 3, pp. 9-530 (Dietz, Berlin 1969), which prints the manuscript with MEW's reconstructions of damaged passages in square brackets and its notices of gaps in angle brackets.

The transcription is mlwerke.de, mirrored at `github.com/wertform/mlwerke.de` (`me/me03/me03_009.htm` to `me03_521.htm`). The authors' own footnotes (marginal notes by Marx and Engels, passages they struck out, and their notes proper) are on the separate page `me03_anm.htm`, numbered (1) to (77) and linked from the text.

**Collation copy.** The zeno.org transcription of MEW 3 (1958 printing), mirrored by MARX-EBOOKS at `github.com/MARX-EBOOKS/marx-ebooks.github.io` (`docs/MEW-ZENO/3/ME03-011.html` to `ME03-521.html`). It has its own slips, so it is not the base, but the two copies were compared word by word over the whole work. The comparison found no passage missing from mlwerke.de beyond the heading and the footnote listed below, and it is the check for every repair.

**Restored from zeno.org:**

- the editorial heading "[B) Mein Verkehr]" (`NT5B.0.1`), which the mlwerke.de page leaves out; it opens section `NT5B`;
- the text of one footnote (`NT5B.N9`, "Vgl. "Die heilige Familie", p. 266."), which mlwerke.de links as a bare "(99)" with no text on the footnote page.

**Repairs** (see `CORRECTIONS` and `NOTE_CORRECTIONS` in `ingest/build_verses.py`). Each agrees with the zeno.org copy. Where the two copies differ and neither is a plain slip, the mlwerke.de reading stands; Berlin dialect forms that Marx and Engels put in Stirner's mouth ("alleene", "Jebildete", "Jleichjültigkeit") are not slips and are kept.

**Words broken across a line or a page.** The transcriber left 57 words split, either at a line end ("aber- gläubige") or around a page number ("Wechsel <18> reiterei"). The ingest joins a pair only when zeno.org has the joined word, and never before "und", "oder", "sein" or "zu"; the list is in `ingest/rejoined.txt`. Five more, which zeno.org does not confirm, are repaired by hand in `CORRECTIONS`.

**Greek.** mlwerke.de sets Greek in the Symbol font, so the HTML holds Latin letters ("sojoz", "h nohsiz"). The ingest puts back the Greek that MEW prints, with accents, checked against zeno.org's transliteration: `AT3.13.3` σοφός, `AT3.15.1` ὥρα, `AT3.23.2` εὐθυμία, `AT3.25.1` συνθήκη, `AT3.28.3` the three phrases from Aristotle, `NT5B.186.2` ὁ θεὸς ἀγάπη ἐστίν and Ἔρως, `NT5B.186.3` ἀγάπη and ἔρως. Two list letters in a table (`AT2.33.1`, `AT2.34.1`) are α and β.

**Footnotes.** The 78 footnotes are `<section>.N<k>`, numbered in order within each section (MEW marks them with asterisks page by page; the mirrors number them through the volume). Each is kept as printed, with MEW's label: "[Im Manuskript gestrichen:]", "[Randbemerkung von Marx:]", "[Randbemerkung von Engels:]" and so on. On the footnote page three numbers sit in odd markup (an anchor left open across a paragraph for (14), "(51" and "(55" with the anchor inside the bracket); the link for (36) points at (35). The ingest reads the number shown, not the link target. Inside footnote (2) (`I.N1.1.1`) a passage the authors struck a second time is marked on mlwerke.de with a strike tag around a bracket; the bracket is kept, the strike is not.

**Tables.** MEW sets Marx and Engels's parodies of Stirner's "equations" as tables. Equation tables are read row by row; a row that begins with "=" or "-" continues the row above, so one chain of equations is one paragraph. Cells that hold only a brace, a dash or "=" are layout and become " = " or " - ". Where MEW prints two texts in parallel columns (Stirner and the authors' corrected version in `AT1.32` to `AT1.35`, `AT4.35` to `AT4.36` and `AT4.105` to `AT4.108`; the two "Bausteine" in `WSI.127` to `WSI.146`; Grün and Cabet in `WSIV.295` to `WSIV.331`), the left column is read first, then the right. Those 124 verses carry `review: true` so the pairing gets checked.

**Normalised in the ingest:** emphasis is dropped; MEW's renderings of foreign phrases in angle brackets (`<Für Heim und Herd>`) and its notices of gaps (`<Hier fehlen im Manuskript 12 Seiten>`) are kept as printed, as in the library's other MEW texts; separator rules ("__________", "*") between parts of a chapter are dropped. MEW's editorial note references (to the endnotes of the volume) are not on the mirrors and are not added.

**Copyright.** Marx died in 1883 and Engels in 1895. The manuscript was first published in 1932 and is in the public domain in Germany and in the United States. MEW's few editorial words (bracketed reconstructions, headings in square brackets, renderings in angle brackets) are kept as printed, as in the library's other MEW texts.

## No English column

The first English translations (1938, parts I and III, and the complete one of 1964 and later) are not public domain in the United States, so none is used. The modern draft is translated straight from the German.

## Sentences

The shared splitter, with rules kept local to this ingest (`_split`): more abbreviations ("Vgl.", "pag.", "sq.", "MM." and others); a page citation standing alone after a quotation ("(p. 17.)", "(ibid.)", "335.") stays with the sentence it cites, as does a short parenthesis such as "(Hegel.)"; a sentence that begins in lower case after a quotation or an ellipsis is not a new sentence; "NB." and "d. h." stay with what follows; a page marker between two sentences moves to the second. Nothing in `scripts/lib` was changed.

Some verses are long because the sentence is long: `I.113.1` is the four numbered results of the materialist conception of history, one sentence after a colon.
