# Sources: Principles of Communism (PC)

## German column

**Text:** MEW 4, pp. 361-380 (Dietz, Berlin), printed from Engels's manuscript, written at the end of October and in November 1847 as a draft programme for the Communist League. Via the mlwerke.de mirror at `github.com/wertform/mlwerke.de` (`me04/me04_361.htm`, UTF-8, without page anchors, so verses carry no MEW page numbers).

**Removed:** the references "(1)" to "(8)" to MEW's editorial notes. The notes are by the MEW editors, not by Engels, and are not included. Two of them record facts about the manuscript worth turning into `commentary/general/` footnotes: Engels left half a page blank for the answer to question 9 (`Q9.0.1`), and for questions 22 and 23 he wrote only "bleibt" ("stays"), meaning the answer was to stay as in an earlier draft (`Q22.1.1`, `Q23.1.1`).

**Second mirror check.** The `MARX-EBOOKS/MLRAW` copy of the same page differs only in markup.

**Copyright.** Engels's words are in the public domain.

## No English column

The standard English translation (Paul Sweezy, 1952) is likely still protected and is not included. The modern draft is translated directly from the German, so this is a good work for contributors who read German.

The earlier "Draft of a Communist Confession of Faith" (June 1847), which has the answers that questions 22 and 23 point back to, could be added later as its own work.

## Sections

`0` is the title. `Q1` to `Q25` are Engels's questions. In each, paragraph 0 is the question and the rest is the answer.
