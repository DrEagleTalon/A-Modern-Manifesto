# Cruxes in The Civil War in France

Good first Discussions. Title the thread with the citation, for example `[CWF III.3.1] the ready-made state machinery`.

| Verse | Question |
|---|---|
| `III.3.1` | "The working class cannot simply lay hold of the ready-made state machinery." Marx and Engels quote this in the 1872 preface to the Manifesto (`CM P1872.2.6`). The modern draft should keep it close enough that the two can be read side by side. |
| `III.4.1` to `III.11.5` | The history of the centralised state from absolute monarchy to the Second Empire in a few long sentences. Split them for a modern reader, but keep the order of the argument. |
| `III.13.1` to `III.22.4` | What the Commune did: the armed people for the standing army, a police stripped of its political functions, elected and revocable officials, workmen's wages for public service. These are the passages readers come for. Plain, exact, no slogans. |
| `III.27.1` | "The political form at last discovered under which to work out the economical emancipation of labor." The most quoted verse after `III.3.1`. "Economical emancipation" needs a modern phrase that still means economic freedom of the working class, not saving money. |
| `III.30.4` to `III.30.6` | "Impossible" and "possible" communism. Marx is mocking his opponents' word; the quotation marks carry the irony and have to survive. |
| `A2.17.11` and `P1891.4.1` | "A war of races" and Engels's "Racenkrieg": in 1870 "race" means nation or people (Slavs and Latins). Translating it as "race" in the modern sense would mislead; "war between peoples" or a note is needed. |
| `III.38.2` to `III.50.1` | Insults of the time, such as "the foul Wallachian" and "cocottes". Decide in Discussions how far the modern draft keeps Marx's sneers and where a note explains them. |
| `IV.42.1` to `IV.45.1` | Marx's defence of the Communards' use of fire. Render the argument as he makes it; the commentary layer is the place for judgment. |
| `IV.46.1` to `IV.49.1` | The shooting of the hostages and of Archbishop Darboy. Marx puts the blame on Thiers. A hard passage for any reader; the modern draft should not soften it or sharpen it. |
| `P1891.17.4` to `P1891.17.7` | Engels on the Commune's respect for the Bank of France, "a serious political error". Short and often quoted. |
| `P1891.21.1` to `P1891.22.5` | Engels's summary of the Commune's two safeguards against officials becoming masters of society. Compare with `III.15.1` to `III.18.2`; the German here is the base for the modern draft, so check it against Marx's English. |
| `P1891.23.6` | "Nichts als eine Maschine zur Unterdrückung einer Klasse durch eine andre" and "Staatsplunder". See the glossary. |
| `P1891.24.1` to `P1891.24.4` | "Diktatur des Proletariats". Keep the term Engels chose; the passage is an answer to people frightened by it. |
| `A1.26.4` | "Whose International rule will be Peace, because its national ruler will be everywhere the same - Labour!" The capitals are part of the rhetoric. |
| `IV.59.1` to `IV.64.1` | The signature block. Names and offices, not prose: leave `modern` empty or copy the names, and check the spellings against a scan (see SOURCES.md). |
